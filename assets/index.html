01 / THE IDEA

Agreement isn’t magic.
It’s a sequence.

Raft makes the journey from an individual proposal to shared state something you can build, inspect, and understand.

01

A leader emerges.

Leader election gives the cluster a place to coordinate. When leadership changes, a new term begins.

02

A proposal travels.

The leader proposes an entry. Members replicate it in their logs, working toward a quorum.

→
03

State moves forward.

Committed entries are applied to state machines. The shared history becomes something useful.
